### Better Hydroponics:
> Adds more readable logic values to hydroponics devices.<br>

#### Logic Values
> Due to limited amount of slot logic variables, there arent many well named varibles,
> so i had to just use whatever wasnt used already.

> Using `ls <r?> <d?> slotIndex logicSlotType<br>
> *<d?> should be a Automated Hydroponics, Hydroponics Device,
> or a Hydroponics Station*<br>
>
> **Temperature**: reads the temperature efficency of plant.<br>
> **Pressure**: reads pressure efficency of plant.<br>
> **PressureAir**: reads breathing efficency of plant.<br>
> **Volume**: reads hydration efficency of plant.<br>
> **Charge**: reads light efficency of plant.<br>
> **On**: reads age of plant.<br>
> **Lock**: reads light stress of plant.<br>
> **Open**: reads lit time ratio of plant.<br>
> **Mode**: reads dark time ratio of plant.<br>
